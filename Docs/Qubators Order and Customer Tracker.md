## **Product Requirements Document (PRD)**

**Product:** Qubators Order and Customer Tracker  
**Target Market:** Nigerian small and micro online sellers  
**Primary Channels:** WhatsApp, Instagram and other social selling channels  
**Product Type:** Order and customer management tool  
**Document Status:** Product Definition

---

# **1\. Product Overview**

Qubators Order and Customer Tracker is a simple business management tool designed for Nigerian online sellers who receive and manage customer orders through WhatsApp, Instagram and other social channels.

Millions of small businesses sell through conversations rather than traditional ecommerce stores. A customer sends a DM, asks for a product, confirms an order, makes a payment and provides delivery details. The seller may then have dozens of other conversations happening at the same time.

This creates a major problem: **orders get forgotten, payments become difficult to track, deliveries are missed and potential repeat customers are lost.**

Qubators gives sellers one organized place to record orders, monitor their progress, track payment status, manage customers and remember who needs a follow-up.

The product should feel less like complicated business software and more like a **simple digital order notebook that never forgets.**

# **2\. Problem Statement**

Small online sellers commonly manage their businesses using:

* WhatsApp conversations  
* Instagram DMs  
* Notes on their phones  
* Paper notebooks  
* Spreadsheets  
* Memory  
* Payment notifications  
* Separate delivery conversations

These methods become difficult to manage as the number of customers increases.

A seller may:

* Forget an order  
* Forget what a customer ordered  
* Lose a customer's contact  
* Forget that payment is still pending  
* Forget to follow up with an interested customer  
* Fail to update a customer after receiving payment  
* Forget which orders have been delivered  
* Lose track of repeat customers  
* Spend too much time searching through old chats

Qubators solves the first and most important problem:

> **"I don't want to lose or forget any customer order."**

# **9\. Order Management**

## **9.1 Add Order**

The primary order-entry method will be **Quick Manual Entry**.

The seller should be able to create an order quickly when a customer places an order through WhatsApp, Instagram or another channel.

### **Order fields**

Each order should contain:

* Customer's name  
* Phone number or WhatsApp contact  
* Product/item ordered  
* Quantity and variations  
* Total order amount  
* Payment status  
* Delivery address or method  
* Order date

The seller should be able to save an order even if some non-essential information is not immediately available.

They should also be able to update the order later.

---

# **10\. Order Status**

Qubators will use a simple order journey:

### **New**

The order has just been recorded.

### **Confirmed**

The seller has confirmed the order with the customer.

### **Processing**

The seller is preparing or fulfilling the order.

### **Delivered**

The customer has received the order.

### **Cancelled**

The order will no longer be fulfilled.

The product should avoid creating too many statuses because excessive complexity can make order management feel like work.

---

# **11\. Payment Tracking**

Payment should be tracked independently from order progress.

Recommended payment statuses:

* **Pending**  
* **Partially Paid**  
* **Paid**  
* **Refunded**

This separation is important.

For example:

> Order Status: Processing  
> Payment Status: Pending

This immediately tells the seller that the order is being handled but money is still outstanding.

---

# **12\. Customer Management**

Every customer connected to an order should have a customer record.

The customer profile should help the seller see:

* Customer name  
* Phone/WhatsApp contact  
* Orders  
* Total purchases  
* Current orders  
* Previous orders  
* Payment history  
* Delivery information  
* Notes  
* Follow-up reminders

The objective is to turn individual conversations into an organized customer history.

---

# **13\. Customer History**

Qubators should allow sellers to see the relationship between a customer and their business.

For example:

**Sarah**

* 5 orders  
* 4 completed  
* 1 current order  
* Total purchases: ₦85,000  
* Last order: September 2026  
* Follow-up: Due tomorrow

This helps sellers recognize repeat customers rather than treating every conversation as a completely new customer.

---

# **14\. Follow-Up Reminders**

One of the major problems with selling through chat is forgetting to follow up.

Qubators should allow sellers to create reminders for situations such as:

* Customer has not confirmed an order  
* Customer has not completed payment  
* Customer requested more information  
* Customer said they would order later  
* Order is awaiting delivery  
* Seller wants to reconnect with a previous customer  
* Customer may be ready for a repeat purchase

The product should make follow-up feel like an organized part of selling rather than an extra administrative task.

# **21\. WhatsApp and Instagram Relationship**

Qubators is designed specifically around businesses that sell through conversations.

However, the initial product should not depend on automatically reading every WhatsApp or Instagram conversation.

The seller sees an order in a conversation and records it in Qubators.

Over time, Qubators can evolve toward deeper integrations and more automated order capture based on customer demand and business opportunity.

# **27\. Product Design Principles**

## **Principle 1: Don't make sellers do unnecessary work**

Qubators exists to reduce administrative work.

## **Principle 2: Capture first, organize later**

Sellers should be able to quickly record an order even when they are busy.

## **Principle 3: Show what needs attention**

The dashboard should focus on actions.

## **Principle 4: Keep language simple**

Avoid complicated business terminology.

## **Principle 5: Mobile-first mindset**

Many target users will manage their businesses primarily through their phones.

## **Principle 6: Build for Nigerian sellers**

The product should understand common realities such as:

* WhatsApp selling  
* Instagram selling  
* Bank transfers  
* Cash payments  
* Delivery riders  
* Customer deposits  
* Balance payments  
* Customers changing delivery details  
* Informal business operations

# **36\. Final Product Definition**

Qubators Order and Customer Tracker is a lightweight customer and order management product for Nigerian online sellers.

Its central purpose is simple:

> **Make sure sellers never lose track of an order or customer simply because the business is happening inside WhatsApp and Instagram conversations.**

The first version should focus heavily on:

**Record → Track → Get Paid → Deliver → Follow Up**

Everything else should support that journey.

The product should not attempt to become a full business-management platform immediately.

Its strongest opportunity is to become the tool a small seller opens every day to answer:

> **"What orders do I have, who has paid, what needs to be delivered, and who do I need to follow up with?"**

If Qubators can answer those questions quickly and reliably, it has a clear foundation for expanding into a much larger social-commerce management platform.
